What makes it different

trust, replaced by verification

Barbell strategy

At least 75% sits in stables at all times; a single momentum sleeve takes at most 22%, with hard stops, a kill switch inside the drawdown limit, and a ratchet that banks gains. Pure deterministic code — the AI can only de-risk.

Decision receipts

Every cycle emits one hash-chained receipt — data purchased, regime, thesis, intents, transactions — and each commits to the previous one's hash. Alter any past decision and every hash after it breaks.

ERC-8004 anchoring

Once a day the chain head is committed on-chain under the agent's ERC-8004 identity. The proof runs end to end: public log → recomputed locally → on-chain commitment.

Metered data, not alpha

The agent buys exactly the data it needs and records the cost in each receipt — capped per call against a session budget, enforced in the signer. Honest scope, surfaced per decision.
