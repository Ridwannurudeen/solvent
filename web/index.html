Self-custodial

Execution runs through the Trust Wallet keychain. Keys never leave the host; every swap is signed locally and verified on-chain before the agent's state changes.

Verifiable by design

Each cycle emits one receipt committing to the prior one's hash. Alter any past decision and every hash after it breaks — and the daily on-chain anchor catches it.

Receipts prove the declared inputs and outputs are hash-bound — not that a specific model ran. It is not a TEE or zk proof.
